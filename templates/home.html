About the Platform

A better way to manage verification

The platform brings instrument owners, Legal Metrology Officers and Government Approved Testing Centres together through one organized digital workflow.

From application to verification record

Verification of weighing and measuring instruments involves several important activities such as application submission, document review, assignment, scheduling, testing and record maintenance.

e-Verification provides a centralized platform where these activities can be managed digitally, reducing unnecessary paperwork and making the verification process easier to monitor.

Every stakeholder can work according to their assigned role while important application and verification information remains organized in the system.

How It Works

Four simple stages

The complete workflow is organized into clear stages so every stakeholder knows what happens next.

The instrument owner creates an account and registers the weighing or measuring instrument.

02

Apply

The owner submits an online verification application with the required information.

03

Review & Test

04

Record

The completed verification information is maintained as a digital official record.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions

A quick overview of how the e-Verification platform works.

What is e-Verification?

e-Verification is a digital platform for managing the application, review, scheduling, testing and record-keeping activities related to verification of weighing and measuring instruments.

Who can use the platform?

The system provides separate workspaces for instrument owners, Legal Metrology Officers and Government Approved Testing Centres.

Can an owner track an application?

Yes. The owner workspace is designed to display submitted verification applications and their current status.

What does an LMO do?

The Legal Metrology Officer reviews applications, manages assignments and coordinates verification activities.

What is the role of a GATC?

A Government Approved Testing Centre handles assigned testing and verification activities and updates the relevant verification records.
